Durga Visarjan 2026 — Ghat water-quality risk brief

11 Oct 2026 · Prepared from MPPCB immersion reports 2023–2025 · Durga visarjan: 20 Oct 2026

Situation

Across MPPCB tests at Bhopal's 7 immersion ghats in 2023–2025, 42 of 116 dissolved-oxygen readings were below the bathing limit of 5 mg/l (4 were 0 mg/l), and 100 of 112 BOD readings were above 3 mg/l. Two ghats have no Durga 2025 test at all, so they must be tested first.

Ranked ghats (highest priority first)

  1. Bairagarh (Sant Hirdaram) kund — INSUFFICIENT DATA (score 0.46) — Dissolved oxygen was 0 mg/l during Ganesh 2025. The bathing limit is at least 5 mg/l. Source: MPPCB 2025 report, page 28. — Test first, Aerate, Clean, Sample metals, NGT lake ban
  2. Malikhedi kund — INSUFFICIENT DATA (score 0.26) — Dissolved oxygen was 0.6 mg/l during Ganesh 2025. The bathing limit is at least 5 mg/l. Source: MPPCB 2025 report, page 29. — Test first, Aerate, Clean
  3. Shahpura lake ghat — HIGH (score 0.27) — BOD was 11.4 mg/l during Durga 2025. The bathing limit is at most 3 mg/l. Source: MPPCB 2025 report, page 65. — Aerate, Clean, Sample metals
  4. Rani Kamlapati ghat / Kamla Park kund — HIGH (score 0.21) — BOD was 8.8 mg/l during Durga 2025. The bathing limit is at most 3 mg/l. Source: MPPCB 2025 report, page 64. — Aerate, Clean, Sample metals, NGT lake ban
  5. Hathaikheda dam / kund — MEDIUM (score 0.2) — BOD was 10 mg/l during Ganesh 2025. The bathing limit is at most 3 mg/l. Source: MPPCB 2025 report, page 29. — Clean, Sample metals
  6. Khatlapura ghat — LOW (score 0.15) — BOD was 11.2 mg/l one week after the Ganesh 2023 immersion. The bathing limit is at most 3 mg/l. Source: MPPCB 2023 report, page 26. — Clean, Sample metals, NGT lake ban
  7. Prempura ghat — LOW (score 0.05) — BOD was 6.8 mg/l during Ganesh 2024. The bathing limit is at most 3 mg/l. Source: MPPCB 2024 report, page 70. — Clean, NGT lake ban
